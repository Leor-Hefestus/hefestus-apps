Priority To QuickBooks

An internal tool of Hefestus Technologies Ltd. Not for public use.

What it does

Priority To QuickBooks is a Windows desktop app used by two staff members of Hefestus Technologies. It connects to one QuickBooks Online company, Hefestus USA. It updates existing items in Products & Services: the sales description and the item picture. It takes both from our Priority ERP system. It never creates items and never reads or changes financial data.

Start the app

The app is installed on company PCs only. Open Priority To QuickBooks from the Windows Start menu. There is no web version.

Connect or reconnect

  1. Open Priority To QuickBooks.
  2. If it shows Sign in to QuickBooks, click it.
  3. Sign in with a QuickBooks admin user of Hefestus USA, pick the company, and click Connect.

The app connects by itself after that. It asks again only on a new PC or Windows user, or when the connection expired or was removed.

Disconnect

A QuickBooks admin of Hefestus USA can remove the connection at any time: in QuickBooks Online, open Apps, then My apps, find this app, and click Disconnect. After that the app can no longer read or change anything in QuickBooks. The saved sign-in on each PC stops working at once.
