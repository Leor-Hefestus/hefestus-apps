Priority To QuickBooks

An internal app of Hefestus Technologies Ltd. Not for public use.

Updates item descriptions and pictures in Hefestus USA's QuickBooks Online.

Start

Open Priority To QuickBooks on a company PC.

Connect

In the app, click Sign in to QuickBooks and follow the steps.

Disconnect

In QuickBooks Online, open Apps, then My apps, and click Disconnect.
